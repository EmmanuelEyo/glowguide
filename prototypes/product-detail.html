Formulated for all skin types, the Lumière Hydrating Serum addresses surface dryness, fine lines, and loss of suppleness in a single, lightweight step.

  • Multi-weight hyaluronic acid — penetrates at multiple skin depths for surface and deeper hydration
  • Tripeptide-5 complex — supports the appearance of firmness and reduces fine lines
  • Panthenol (Pro-Vitamin B5) — soothes, repairs, and locks in moisture
  • Free from parabens, synthetic fragrance, and sulfates
  • Clinically tested for skin tolerance

Full ingredient list (INCI):

Aqua, Sodium Hyaluronate (High Molecular Weight), Sodium Hyaluronate (Low Molecular Weight), Hydrolysed Sodium Hyaluronate, Tripeptide-5, Glycerin, Niacinamide, Panthenol, Allantoin, Tocopherol, Citric Acid, Sodium Citrate, Carbomer, Phenoxyethanol, Ethylhexylglycerin.

Apply 3–4 drops to clean, slightly damp skin morning and evening. Press gently into the skin using your fingertips, working from the centre of your face outward.

Follow immediately with your moisturiser to seal in hydration. For best results, use consistently for 4–6 weeks. Suitable for use under SPF and makeup.

Avoid the immediate eye area. If irritation occurs, discontinue use.
